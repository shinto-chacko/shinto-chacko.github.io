Explore the app by role

One society. Three everyday roles.

DoorNotice brings the committee, the gate and residents into one practical flow, with tools shaped around what each person needs to do.

For the committee

Keep society updates and follow-up in view.

Bring notices, approvals and society records into one committee workflow. Choose who receives an update and keep follow-up easy to find.

Committee workspace

  • Targeted noticesPost to all residents or selected flats, with attachments.
  • Owner approvalsReview pending requests and keep decisions moving.
  • Incident follow-upCheck resident-reported issues and their status.
  • Flat and vehicle searchFind society records when the committee needs them.
  • Security logsReview gate activity alongside society updates.
  • Access controlsManage access for the society workspace.
For security staff

Handle the gate with a clear log.

Record who enters and leaves, and keep visitor and daily-help activity together for the society.

At the gate

  • Visitor entryRecord visitor arrivals and check-outs.
  • Daily-help recordsTrack domestic-help entry and check-out activity.
  • Multi-flat check-inCheck in daily help visiting several flats in sequence.
  • Security logKeep a clear record of gate activity for follow-up.
For owners and residents

See updates. Respond when needed.

Keep useful society information close, respond to requests and follow up on issues from your resident view.

Resident view

  • Visitor approvalsReview visitor requests and respond when needed.
  • Society noticesRead updates and attachments shared by the committee.
  • BillsView bills uploaded to DoorNotice by the society.
  • Incident reportingReport an issue and check its status follow-up.
03 / 03 · Owners and residents

Useful features, without the extra clutter.

DoorNotice focuses on notices, security handling, visitor and daily-help records, bill visibility and resident services. The aim is a clear day-to-day experience for committee members, security staff and owners, without advertising clutter or unnecessary screens.

Optional billing add-on · DoorDues

Need more control over maintenance billing?

DoorDues is the separate billing workspace for society setup, maintenance bill generation, offline payment records and dues reports. Add it when your committee wants a dedicated billing tool alongside DoorNotice.
